# Demo video script — English, under three minutes

Record the actual app with its fictional example and fictional bundled PDF only. Do not show credentials, inboxes, real personal data or unrelated screens. Use no unlicensed music. Show the source lines rather than treating labels as conclusions.

1. **The problem** (0:00–0:15): Creators face lengthy calls and need to know what to review before investing time.
2. **Try the example** (0:15–0:45): Open the independent Compass, select the fictional example and show the candidate date and its source. “The tool proposes what to check; you confirm the source.”
3. **Terms and changes** (0:45–1:15): Show the evidence lens, a rights alert and the literal added/removed lines. Filter by topic. “Labels help you find a passage; they do not interpret it.”
4. **Turn review into action** (1:15–1:45): Confirm the date, show the planning timeline, checklist and calendar reference. Mention the local PDF import.
5. **How it was built** (1:45–2:15): Show the `.kiro/` specs and a local test run. “Kiro CLI helped develop and test the calendar regression and change-review feature; its output was reviewed and corrected. The app itself has no runtime AI.”
6. **Close** (2:15–2:30): “An independent DUA prototype for source-based decisions, with a possible future connection to Radar Cultural.” State that patterns can miss details and every notice needs human review.
